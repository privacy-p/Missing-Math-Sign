# Privacy Policy — Missing Math Sign

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Missing Math Sign**.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how Missing Math Sign handles information when you use our mobile game.

Missing Math Sign is an offline educational puzzle game designed to challenge your mathematical reasoning skills.

**Contact email:** contact@emerworx.online

## 2. Information We Collect

Missing Math Sign does not collect, transmit, or share personal information.

Specifically, the game does not collect:

- Names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
- Location information.
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs.
- Financial or payment information.
- Personal usage analytics.
- Contacts, photos, or other personal files.

No registration or account creation is required to play.

## 3. Locally Stored Game Data

Missing Math Sign saves certain gameplay information locally on your device to provide game functionality.

This may include:

- Game scores and personal bests.
- Completed puzzle levels and progression.
- Stars, streaks, and achievements.
- Virtual coin balances and hint usage.
- Daily puzzle progress.
- Game settings and preferences.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties.

You can delete your saved game progress using the **Settings → Reset progress** option within the game.

Uninstalling the application generally removes its locally stored data, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings.

## 4. Advertising and In-App Purchases

Missing Math Sign:

- Does not display advertisements.
- Does not contain advertising SDKs.
- Does not offer in-app purchases.
- Does not require payments or subscriptions.

Any virtual coins earned or used within the game are exclusively for gameplay purposes. They have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real money.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Missing Math Sign does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

We do not sell, rent, or share user information with advertisers, marketing companies, or data brokers.

## 6. Internet Connectivity

Missing Math Sign is designed to work offline.

An internet connection is not required to access the game's core features, including timed challenges, Puzzle Quest, and daily puzzles.

The application does not transmit gameplay information or personal data to our servers.

## 7. Data Security

Because Missing Math Sign does not collect or transmit user data, we do not maintain a remote database containing players' personal information.

Game progress is stored locally on the user's device.

Users are encouraged to keep their devices secure and updated.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Missing Math Sign is a mathematics puzzle game that can be enjoyed by players of different ages.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

It does not require users to provide their names, contact details, or other personal information.

If you have concerns regarding children's privacy, please contact us using the email address below.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not retain personal information on external servers.

Locally saved game progress remains on your device until it is deleted or reset, subject to the device's storage and backup behavior.

To reset your gameplay progress:

1. Open Missing Math Sign.
2. Navigate to **Settings**.
3. Select **Reset progress**.

You may also uninstall the application to remove its locally stored application data, subject to your device's backup settings.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information on our servers, we do not maintain user profiles or personal data records.

You can manage or delete locally saved game progress directly through the application.

If you have any privacy-related questions or requests, you may contact us.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the application, its features, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
